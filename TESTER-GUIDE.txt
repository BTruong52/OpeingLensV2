OpeingLensV3 - WINDOWS TEST BUILD (3.0.0)

START HERE
Extract the ENTIRE ZIP, then open OpeingLensV3.exe inside the extracted folder.
Keep all files together. Windows 10 (2004+) or Windows 11, x64.
No Node.js, installer, browser extension, or admin access required.
This is an unsigned portable test build.

QUICK TEST
1. Open a chess analysis board or bot practice game.
2. Click Auto-find board & calibrate. Adjust and confirm the board and Moves
   panel boxes. Manual selection is available if detection fails.
3. Check that Playing as matches your bottom pieces. Test White AND Black.
   Pick a skill level and one of the three suggested openings/defenses.
4. The floating Coach and arrows should appear automatically. An empty move
   panel at the starting position should not block the first recommendation.
5. Play several moves, including captures. Check that the board preview,
   position, Coach text and arrows stay synchronized. Try a slow piece drag.
6. Try an unexpected reply: look for optional variation switches or position
   plans. Confirm that arrows match the board orientation.
7. Reset for new game: the selected opening stays, the move-panel crop clears,
   and the board crop stays. Select the move panel again if using it.
8. Discord: open Streaming > Discord > Open Stream View. Share the window
   called Opening Lens Stream View in Discord. Keep it on another monitor
   or outside the board and Moves panel crops. Check that viewers see the
   board, arrows and current Coach explanation. Tracking must be running.
   Stream View shows the board and coaching, not the entire game interface.

REPORTING A BUG
Before reproducing: expand Developer Mode near the bottom and enable it.
After the problem: choose Last failure to see what OLens captured and read.
Click Export diagnostics and send that JSON with:
- Windows version, chess site/app, and monitor scaling (100%, 125%, etc.).
- Whether playing White or Black, skill level and selected opening.
- Moves played and steps needed to reproduce the issue.
- What you expected versus what happened; include the displayed status.
- For streaming issues: Discord share source and what viewers actually saw.
Review exported images before sharing; do not send tokens or account details.
Developer Mode starts off each session and may add overhead while recording.

KNOWN LIMITS
Recognition varies with board themes, small text, piece icons, scrolling and
animations. Ambiguous positions may wait for confirmation. Keep capture
areas unobstructed. Calibration and streaming need testing on your setup.
Lichess online continuations are optional and require your own token; core
catalog, OCR and Coach work offline. Use coaching where assistance is allowed.

Third-party notices ship with the app. Stockfish.js licensing and source
information are in resources/app/engine.

V3 REGRESSION CHECKS
Try two moves between confirmations, pawn captures such as cxd5 versus exd5,
and a scrolling late-game move list. Older OCR errors should not block new
moves once the current position is confirmed. Recovery still requires a
unique legal position supported by piece shapes.
