# Lichess chess opening names

The five TSV files and COPYING.txt are from [lichess-org/chess-openings](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings/tree/c67912be581f0793dbaa776be5ccf111e01f88d9), revision `c67912be581f0793dbaa776be5ccf111e01f88d9`, under CC0 1.0.

All 3,815 supplied line records are included. This is a catalog of named opening lines, separate from the Lichess database of played games. Opening Lens builds position and continuation indexes locally by replaying the TSV lines.
